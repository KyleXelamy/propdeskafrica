I-MANAGE BY SIEMAX LTD

About
I-Manage is a property management platform for landlords, property managers and agencies. It brings property records, tenants, rent collection, maintenance, documents and reporting into one workspace.

Company
Siemax Ltd
Product: I-Manage
Base: Nairobi, Kenya
Website: https://i-manage.app

What the platform helps with
- Organising properties and tenant records
- Tracking leases, rent payments and expenses
- Managing maintenance requests and documents
- Supporting M-Pesa-native payment workflows
- Producing clearer portfolio reports and exports
