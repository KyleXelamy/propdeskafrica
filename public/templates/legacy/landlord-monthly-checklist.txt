PROPDESK AFRICA BY SIEMAX LTD
LANDLORD MONTHLY CHECKLIST

About twenty minutes, once a month. The cheapest risk control a landlord
has. Work down the list and note anything you carry into next month.

LANDLORD: ______________________________     MONTH: _________________

1. RENT COLLECTION
☐  Send rent reminders to every tenant on the 1st.
☐  Chase tenants who have not paid by the 5th, in writing.
☐  Record every payment in PropDesk Africa on the day it arrives.
☐  Issue a receipt for each payment received.
☐  Reconcile M-Pesa and bank statements against the ledger.
☐  Review arrears aging; decide on written demands for persistent
    non-payers.

2. PROPERTY CONDITION
☐  Walk the premises and common areas.
☐  Review open maintenance requests; close out what is done.
☐  Check for leaks, damp, pests and roof damage after the rains.
☐  Confirm gates, locks, lighting and water tanks are secure.
☐  Verify the fire extinguisher and smoke alarms are present and in date.

3. LEASES AND NOTICES
☐  Diary the end of each fixed term — six months and three months out.
☐  Confirm all notices were served in writing and proof is filed.
☐  Check no notice period given is shorter than the Agreement requires.
☐  Renew expiring leases in writing rather than letting them roll over.

4. MONEY AND TAX
☐  Reconcile monthly income against the property's expected rent.
☐  Set aside funds for KRA income tax on schedule.
☐  File the KRA return when due; keep the computation.
☐  Review expenses claimed against receipts held.
☐  Set aside for county property rates and any service charge.

5. COMPLIANCE
☐  Confirm buildings insurance is valid and covers current values.
☐  Check certificate and permit renewals falling due this month.
☐  Review NHIF or NSSF obligations for a caretaker or agent.
☐  Confirm the fire risk assessment and any drill requirement is current.

6. PERSONAL DATA
☐  Delete or anonymise tenant records for ended tenancies where no dispute
    is live.
☐  Confirm access to tenant ID numbers and payment records is limited to
    those who need it.
