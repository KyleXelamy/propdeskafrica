- The tenant accepts the condition recorded and undertakes to return the

WHY THIS DOCUMENT MATTERS
- Damage not recorded here is, in practice, damage you cannot charge for.
- Photograph every defect on move-in, even minor ones. A move-out
  comparison is worthless without a baseline.
- Both signatures matter. An unsigned report is a private note, not an
  agreed record.
- If the tenant cannot be present, note that above and photograph the same
  day.
