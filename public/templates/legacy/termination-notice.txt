WHAT THE TENANT MUST DO BY THE TERMINATION DATE
- Vacate and return all keys, remotes and access cards listed on the
  handover record.
- Leave the premises clean and in the condition recorded at move-in, fair
  wear and tear excepted.
- Settle rent due to the termination date and any utility charges properly
  outstanding.
- Provide a forwarding address so the deposit and post can be returned.
- Attend a joint move-out inspection, or propose a date in writing within
  7 days of this notice.

THE DEPOSIT
Within 30 days of vacating and returning the keys, the refundable balance
will be paid with a written itemised statement of deductions and supporting
evidence. Permitted deductions: unpaid rent, evidenced utility arrears, and
damage beyond fair wear and tear compared against the signed move-in
condition report. Normal wear and tear is not damage and is not chargeable.

BEFORE YOU SERVE THIS — IMPORTANT
- Give at least one full calendar month, expiring at the end of a tenancy
  month. A shorter period is open to challenge.
- Serve in writing: by hand, registered post, or email to the address most
  recently notified. Record how and when you served it.
- If rent is outstanding, this notice does NOT recover possession. You must
  apply to the Rent Restriction Tribunal or the court for an order.
- Never change the locks, remove the tenant's goods, or cut off water or
  electricity to obtain possession. Self-help eviction is unlawful and is a
  criminal offence.
- Give a copy of any notice to the Rent Restriction Tribunal for the area.
