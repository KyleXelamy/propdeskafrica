SAFETY-CRITICAL COMPLAINTS
- Fire, gas, structural collapse, exposed live wiring, flooding, or a
  threat to a person: make the premises safe FIRST, then report to the
  police and the relevant authority, and record the time of each call.
- Eviction threats, harassment, or a utility disconnection: record the
  exact words used, the date and time, and the names of anyone present.
  These are matters for the Tribunal or the police, not for a letter.
- Never resolve a complaint by disconnecting a service or by removing a
  tenant's property. Both are unlawful.
